Stock-room handheld

The staff side of the YugeSavers prototype

This is the half of the idea shoppers never see. The handheld lists stock that is about to go out of date. One press turns it into a meal bundle.

  1. 1

    Tick the lines to use. A line due today counts 3, tomorrow 2, in two days 1.

  2. 2

    Generate. The recipe whose ingredients add up to the highest count wins. The handheld shows the scores so you can check it.

  3. 3

    Publish. The price is 50% off if anything is due today, 35% for tomorrow, 25% for two days, rounded down to the nearest 5p. The bundle then appears in the shopper app.

Stock, prices and recipes are invented demo data, kept in this browser only.
