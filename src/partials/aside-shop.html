YugeSavers

Rescue bundles for food that is close to its date

A supermarket app prototype. Store staff turn stock that is about to go out of date into a discounted meal bundle with a recipe, and shoppers see it in the app while there is still time to cook it.

  1. 1

    Staff check what is close to its date. The stock-room handheld lists every near-expiry line and how many units are left.

  2. 2

    A rule builds the bundle. It picks the recipe that uses the most urgent stock and prices it 25% to 50% below shelf price.

  3. 3

    Shoppers see a reduced sticker. The bundle shows up in the app, and the number left drops as people add it to their basket.

Try it: open the staff door, publish a bundle, then come back to the shop. It all runs in this browser. The store, the shopper, the prices and the stock are invented.
